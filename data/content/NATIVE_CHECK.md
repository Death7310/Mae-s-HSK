# For the native-speaker check: look at these first

Everything in the app was written and reviewed by language models. These are
the items the authors and reviewers themselves were least sure of when writing
HSK 5–6 (words, dialogues, grammar). The label is the batch or part the item is
in; `author` and `review` say who raised it.

One convention to decide for the whole app: the measure word 场 for rain,
disasters, accidents and wars is written `chǎng` in nine word examples and
`cháng` in one. 《现代汉语词典》 gives `cháng` for events that run their course
(一场雨、一场病、一场大战) and `chǎng` for performances and matches.
